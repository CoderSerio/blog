I’m studying for IELTS, and I want to keep my course notes, questions, and my teacher’s advice in one place. This series is also a chance to practise writing in English. When I come back to a note, I want to see what I understand now and what I still need to work on.

## What will I keep here?

- **Course notes**: what we covered in class, useful examples, and my understanding of the lesson.
- **Questions and mistakes**: my original answer, where I got stuck, and what helped me understand or revise it.
- **Teacher feedback**: the advice I received on a particular exercise and how I plan to use it.

The series will cover listening, reading, writing, and speaking. Each post can focus on one lesson, one question, or one piece of feedback, with tags for the relevant skill.

## How will I organise a note?

I want to review each lesson through a conversation with an AI interviewer. The interviewer asks one question at a time, and I share what I remember in English, Chinese, or both. The post keeps both sides of the conversation, so the questions and any clarifications remain alongside my answers.

> 用访谈来回顾每节课：一次问一个问题，按我的回答继续追问。保留双方的发言，先记录，后润色；不替我补出还没讲到的经历、答案或知识点。根据旧内容重新组织的问答会注明是整理版，不当作逐字实录。

I want to keep the steps visible: **the task → my answer or question → explanation and feedback → my revision → further practice**.

| Part | What to record |
| --- | --- |
| Context | The date, lesson topic, source, or task |
| First attempt | My original answer, wording, or question |
| Explanation and feedback | What the teacher said, my own understanding, and any suggested corrections, with their sources identified |
| Revision | The original and revised sentences, with a short explanation of the changes |
| Next practice | Something specific to practise, followed by what I notice when I try again |

Not every post needs every part. If a question is still open, I can mark it as “to be confirmed” and return to it later.

> 整理时保留原始答案，区分老师的原话、自己的理解和辅助修改建议。暂时没弄懂的地方可以先留着。

## Writing is part of the practice

I’ll write mainly in English and use Chinese when it helps me explain a difficult point or capture an idea I cannot express yet. I can return to those Chinese notes and try writing them in English later.

When I ask for help with a draft, I want to keep my first attempt alongside the suggested revision. A short explanation of the most useful changes will help me try those expressions in my next piece of writing.

> 英文为主，中文辅助。先尝试自己写，再对照修改；每次挑几个值得练习的表达，放到下一篇里用。

I’ll distinguish direct quotes from my teacher from my own summaries. That way, I can trace a suggestion back to the exercise and understand why I made a change.

## Capture first, polish later

When I talk through a lesson, I may switch between English and Chinese. I want to practise moving between the two languages and become more comfortable communicating in an international company in the future.

For now, I want a rough record that stays close to what I say. Small corrections are welcome, but the detailed editing can come later. If something is unclear, I want to keep the original wording and mark it as a question rather than guess what it means.

> 先按我说的记下来，能理解就好。保留中英文切换和原始表达，不急着改成完整、流畅的文章；之后再一起细改。

Only include lesson content I have actually described. Wait for me to explain what we learned before adding any learning points. Keep vocabulary and grammar notes next to the relevant words in clearly labelled blockquotes, with Chinese explanations and English examples. Include points from our review conversations too, especially those I emphasize, misunderstand, or repeatedly get wrong.

> 课程笔记只记录我已经讲到的内容，不提前补充知识点、关键词或课堂活动。针对我实际表达的词汇和语法，用就近的引用块记录，保留原始表达、中文意思和英文用法，并注明这是回顾交流中的语言笔记。用中文代替的词也要补上英文。我强调、容易误解或反复用错的语法要留下来；同类错误再次出现时可以标注，之后再集中复习。

我还不能一开始就把所有内容都写成英文，需要一些时间适应。暂时说不清的意思可以先用中文记下来，之后再尝试用英文表达。英文为主是逐步练习的方向，不是每篇笔记都必须达到的比例。

## Keeping notes after class

My teacher told me that classes are on Thursdays, Fridays, and Saturdays. I plan to attend when I’m free. I want to write notes immediately after each class, while I can still remember what I learned.

**My original words — not yet polished:**

> And for the content of the articles, I want to read them. I want to read them in lesson. You know, there are many lessons. The teacher told me every Thursday, Friday and Saturday. I will take these classes. I will take these classes if I don't have other things to do. Yeah, if I'm free, I will handle this. But by the way, I think I'd rather some notes after the class, write immediately, for otherwise I will forget what I have learned in the class.

**To clarify later:** “I want to read them in lesson.” 这里是指在课堂上读笔记，还是按每节课整理文章？先保留原句，等确认后再改。

**Interviewer（AI）：What was the topic of the first lesson?**

### 1. The diving man

**Interviewer（AI）：What did you start with in class?**

**Carbon：** At the start of the class, the first topic was a man diving. The teacher asked what I thought he was feeling and why. This is how I recalled the question:

> How do I think he feels and why?

**Interviewer（AI）：What did you notice when you answered?**

**Carbon：** The teacher understood what I wanted to say, but I noticed a problem: I couldn’t speak fluently. I repeated the same word again and again, and it sounded strange to me.

It felt like “结巴”. I didn’t know how to express that in English. I was nervous, and that made it even harder to speak fluently. It felt awful.

> **“结巴／说话磕磕绊绊”（回顾交流中的语言笔记）**
>
> 我当时想表达的是：回答时会反复重复同一个词，说不流畅，有点像中文说的“结巴”，而且越紧张越难说清楚。
>
> 可以这样描述这次说话的状态：
>
> I kept stumbling over my words.
>
> 我说话总是磕磕绊绊。

I think part of the reason was that I couldn’t say the words clearly and loudly. When I watched the recording of myself, I found it hard to understand what I had said.

**Interviewer（AI）：** 这里先记下你自己的感受和对原因的猜测，不把它写成老师的评价。具体回答和后续内容等你继续回忆后再补充。

### 继续回顾：Two difficulties I noticed

**Interviewer（AI）：When the teacher asked how the man felt, what did you want to say?**

老师问那个潜水的人是什么感受时，你当时想表达什么？不用还原完整英文，先把意思说出来就好。

**Carbon（原始表达）：**

> **开始使用刚学到的表达（回顾交流中的语言笔记）**
>
> 在后面的回顾里，我开始尝试使用这句话，也出现了自我纠正：
>
> I kept my words stumbling. → I kept stumbling over my words.
>
> 后一句是这里自然的说法。把这段尝试留下来，也能看见我怎样开始使用刚学到的表达。

> **两个“问题”：questions 还是 difficulties？（回顾交流中的语言笔记）**
>
> 我说了 **“I found two questions about this.”**，这里指的是组织回答和自然表达上的两个困难，可以说：
>
> I noticed two difficulties.
>
> 我注意到了两个困难。
>
> **Questions** 更适合指想问的问题；这里用 **difficulties** 更贴近我的意思。这是针对复述表达的语言建议，不是课堂知识点。

First, I can't just answer the question in a structured way. You know, for example, a structured way should be, like, first should be, like summary, and then just give some points in order: first, second, and then finally, that sort of thing. And then the summary all in all, like so.

And second, and another, for the, you know, and second, I can't express my meaning in a natural way. Somehow maybe my vocabulary is poor, which makes this kind of situation, and maybe some part is for the habit. I didn't notice how the native speakers use their language to express.

**Interviewer（AI）：** 你这次把困难分成了两点：

1. **组织回答**：你希望先概括，再依次展开几点，最后总结，但说的时候还做不到。这是你希望采用的组织方式。
2. **自然表达**：你觉得可能与词汇量、表达习惯有关，也注意到自己过去没有太留意母语者怎样表达。这些原因先保留为你的猜测。

> 这一段先记录回答时的困难；关于潜水者的具体回答，你还没有讲到。

### Starting before I know what to say

**Interviewer（AI）：When you got stuck, did you already know what you wanted to say in Chinese?**

你卡住的时候，是中文意思已经清楚、但不知道怎么用英文说，还是当时也没想好要说什么？

**Carbon（原始表达）：**

Ah, you remind me that I have a bad habit, that I always open my mouth to speak something, even though I have not. I have not think what should I say. Yeah, so it's causing the result. I always speak something in a big mess.

> **还没想好，就开始说了（回顾交流中的语言笔记）**
>
> 我的原话是 **“I have not think what should I say”** 和 **“I always speak something in a big mess.”** 想表达的是：还没想好要说什么就开口，结果说得很乱。
>
> 整理时的表达建议：
>
> I often start speaking before I’ve thought about what I want to say, so my answer comes out jumbled.
>
> 我经常还没想好要说什么就开始说，结果回答就乱了。
>
> 这里先记两个小地方：
>
> - **have not think → have not thought**：在这个结构中，**have** 后面要用 **thought**。
> - **what I should say**：放在句子里面表示“我该说什么”时，用陈述语序；单独提问才是 **“What should I say?”**
>
> 建议句用 **what I want to say** 表达“我想说什么”，用 **comes out jumbled** 描述说出来的内容缺乏条理。这些是对我复述表达的修改建议，不是老师的课堂反馈。

**Interviewer（AI）：** 你补充了一个具体的观察：有时还没想好要说什么，就已经开始说了，结果表达容易乱。这先作为你的自我观察记录，不认定它解释了所有卡顿。

**Interviewer（AI）：Does this happen when you speak Chinese too, or mainly when you speak English?**

说中文时，你也会这样，还是主要在说英文时出现？

**Carbon（原始表达）：**

Yeah, actually, it's the same when I speak in Chinese. I made the same mistakes. I always make the same mistakes. But I'm trying to correct this kind of... I try to correct it. But it's much harder in English.

> **我正在尝试改掉这个习惯（回顾交流中的语言笔记）**
>
> 我说 **“I'm trying to correct this kind of... I try to correct it.”** 时，想表达的是：说中文时也有这个习惯，我正在尝试改，但说英文时更难。
>
> 可以轻微调整为：
>
> It happens when I speak Chinese too. I’m trying to change this habit, but it’s much harder when I speak English.
>
> 我说中文时也会这样。我正在尝试改变这个习惯，但说英文时更难。
>
> 这里用 **change this habit** 表达“改变这个习惯”；**I’m trying to…** 保留“正在尝试”的意思，不表示已经改好了。

**Interviewer（AI）：** 明白，说中文时也会出现这种情况，你正在尝试改，但说英文时更难。

### 2. Have you ever tried diving or swimming?

**Carbon（继续回忆，原始表达）：**

So just with the better to, to the second question the teacher asked me. It's, and it's, have me, have I? He asked, Have you ever tried diving, or have you ever tried to dive or swim before?

I said, I, no, I have never experienced diving before, but I tried to swim and I take a lot of lessons about this. In fact, it's about my six-year-old, and I have already come to the skill. I'm not sure whether it's right to express like so, because there are some vocabulary I don't know, such as 时态. You know, there are lots of 时态 in English. 过去完成时什么之类的. I'm not sure what exactly it is in English. I mean, I don't know how to speak the words, not what is it.

> **“时态”和“过去完成时”（回顾交流中的语言笔记）**
>
> 这次我问的是英文术语怎么说，不是要求解释这些语法概念：
>
> - **时态：tense**；复数是 **tenses**。
> - **过去完成时：the past perfect tense**，也可以简称 **the past perfect**。
>
> 这一轮先记名称；后面确认了年龄和时间关系，再讨论具体句子用什么时态。

**Interviewer（AI）：** 你记得老师问了潜水或游泳的经历，具体原话暂时保留你回忆中的两种说法。你的回答提到了没有潜水经历，以及上过不少游泳课。

**后续确认（按话题放在这里）：**

**Interviewer（AI）：Did you mean you learned to swim when you were about six?**

你是想说“大约六岁时学会了游泳”吗？

**Carbon（原始表达）：**

Oh yes, that's what I want to see. Did I, oh, learn to swim when I was about six. So what's different? What's wrong with my previous expression? Is there anything

**Interviewer（AI）：** 明白，你确认的是“大约六岁时学会了游泳”。

> **从原句到确认后的表达（回顾交流中的语言笔记）**
>
> 原句：**“it's about my six-year-old, and I have already come to the skill.”**
>
> 确认意思后，可以说：**“I learned to swim when I was about six.”**（我大约六岁时学会了游泳。）
>
> **1. 年龄：when I was about six。** **My six-year-old** 通常指“我六岁的孩子”，不能表达“我六岁时”。这里需要 **when I was about six**，也可以说 **when I was about six years old**。
>
> **2. 学会游泳：learn to swim。** **Come to the skill** 不是表达“学会这项技能”的自然搭配。说具体学会了什么，用 **learn to + 动词原形** 就够了。
>
> **3. 时态：learned，一般过去时（the past simple tense）。** **When I was about six** 指明了过去的时间，所以这里用 **learned**，不用 **have learned**。这句话也不需要过去完成时。
>
> 结合前面“从未潜过水”的回答，可以说：**“I’ve never been diving, but I learned to swim when I was about six.”** 前半句说截至现在的经历，用现在完成时（**the present perfect tense**）；后半句说六岁左右发生的事，用一般过去时。
>
> 如果想表达的是“到六岁时就已经学会了”，则是另一层时间关系：**“By the time I was six, I had already learned to swim.”** 这里 **had learned** 是过去完成时，强调在那个过去时间之前已学会。它和“大约六岁时学会”意思不同，不能直接替换。

> **这次提问里的两个小表达（回顾交流中的语言笔记）**
>
> 想说“这就是我想表达的意思”，可以用 **“That’s what I meant.”**；这里不是 **“what I want to see”**（我想看到的东西）。
>
> 想问“两种表达有什么区别”，可以用 **“What’s the difference?”**。原来的 **“What’s different?”** 也成立，更侧重问“哪里不一样”。

### 3. The favourite part of a trip

**Carbon（原始表达）：**

And I start to hesitate myself. Maybe I'm a boring and nerdy guy because I don't know how to find the interesting topic way I and my teacher can talk them over. So I just speak something embarrassed, you know?

Like, for example, the teacher asked me, What's the favorite, what's the favorite part of the trip? I think. And the only answer I can speak is that I'm not sure which part is the best. I didn't notice about this. I think every part of this is okay. I think it's not a problem for most people.

**Interviewer（AI）：** 你记得老师还问了旅行中最喜欢的部分，但当时想不出哪一部分最好，觉得每一部分都还可以。你也提到，找不到能和老师展开聊的话题时，会觉得尴尬、开始怀疑自己。这里保留的是你当时的感受，不把它当作对你性格的结论。

> 老师问题的准确措辞暂时保留回忆中的说法。具体是哪次旅行，在下面的问答中继续确认。

**Interviewer（AI）：When your teacher asked about your favourite part of the trip, which trip were you thinking of?**

老师问你旅行中最喜欢的部分时，你当时想到的是哪一次旅行？先说说去了哪里就好，不用急着选出“最喜欢”的部分。

**Carbon（原始表达）：**

When my teacher asked me which part of the trip is my favorite, I answer. When my teacher asked me which part of the trip is my favorite, I think of the trip to Osaka, Japan. It's wonderful, but I found another question about the answering. I don't know. I didn't know how to express my feeling in much more... express my feeling in free vocabulary. You know, this is a very limited vocabulary. The only word I could use was fun, good, wonderful, brilliant, and so on. You know, I don't know how to just expand. I didn't know how to expand the topic, make it much wider.

> **更丰富的词汇、展开回答（回顾交流中的语言笔记）**
>
> 我用了 **“express my feeling in free vocabulary”**，想表达的是：能用的词比较有限，不知道怎样用更多不同的词描述感受。这里可以用 **a wider range of words**（更丰富的词汇／更多不同的词）。
>
> **“I didn’t know how to describe my feelings using a wider range of words.”**（我不知道怎样用更丰富的词汇描述自己的感受。）
>
> 我说的 **“expand the topic, make it much wider”**，在这里主要是想把回答继续展开，可以说 **develop my answer** 或 **add more detail**。
>
> **“I didn’t know how to develop my answer or add more detail.”**（我不知道怎样展开回答，或补充更多细节。）
>
> **Fun、good、wonderful、brilliant** 本身都可以使用；我们接下来会从具体回忆中找细节，不先替这次旅行添加经历或感受。

> **再次遇到的过去时，以及单复数（回顾交流中的语言笔记）**
>
> 回忆老师提问时自己想到什么，用 **“When my teacher asked me, I thought of my trip to Osaka, Japan.”** 这里 **think → thought**，和前面谈“六岁时学会游泳”一样，是在描述过去发生的事。
>
> 原句 **“The only word I could use was fun, good, wonderful, brilliant…”** 后面列了多个词，应配合复数：**“The only words I could think of were ‘fun’, ‘good’, ‘wonderful’, and ‘brilliant’.”**（我当时能想到的只有这些词。）这里把 **word / was** 改为 **words / were**；**could think of** 表示“当时能想到”。

**Interviewer（AI）：** 这次明确了：你想到的是去日本大阪的旅行。你觉得旅行很棒，但描述感受时，常停留在这些词上，也不知道怎样继续展开。

### 课后重试：Talking through the Japan trip

> 这是回顾交流中重新尝试的回答，不是原来的课堂回答。保留原话和自我修正，只按内容分段；语言笔记是 AI 的辅助建议。

**Interviewer（AI）：What’s one thing you remember doing in Osaka?**

你还记得在大阪做过什么事？随便选一件，不需要是最特别的，中英文都可以。

**Carbon（原始表达）：**

Okay, it seems like you are trying to play the role as a teacher and help me to think the question once more. So then I can answer it well, much better than before. Well, let's think for a while and I will try to make it better than what I did before. Okay, there is a summary in one word: it's a very interesting trip.

> **用一句话概括（回顾交流中的语言笔记）**
>
> **In one word** 是“用一个词”，而后面的 **“It’s a very interesting trip”** 是一句话。这里可以用 **“In short, it was a very interesting trip.”**（简而言之，那是一次很有意思的旅行。）

**Carbon（继续）：**

First, we take the airplane from Shanghai to Osaka, and we landed in night. So, and then we took a subway or took a train to the city zone and got my hotel and relaxed for one night there. And the next morning, as the next morning, we go outside and take a round to enjoy the food locally and trying to speak and interact with other people in Japan. It's very interesting, but there are lots of challenges as well, such as we can't speak Japanese. So there are lots of local guys didn't understand what we said. So we need to assist with, so if we wanted to achieve our goal, we assist with our gesture and posture and other things like that. So, and luckily we had the translate software, so it's okay for us to travel around.

> **抵达、逛街和沟通（回顾交流中的语言笔记）**
>
> - **landed in night → landed at night**：在夜间落地，通常说 **at night**。
> - **city zone → the city centre**：如果这里指市中心，可以说 **“We took a train to the city centre.”** 你暂时不确定是地铁还是其他列车，笔记里保留这个不确定之处。
> - **take a round → walk around**：如果指四处走走，可以说 **“We walked around and tried the local food.”**（我们四处逛逛，尝了当地食物。）
> - **gesture and posture → gestures**：这里主要是在描述用手势帮助沟通，可以说 **“We used gestures to help people understand us.”** **Posture** 更常指身体姿态，不是这里必须补上的词。
> - **translate software → translation software / a translation app**：翻译软件／翻译应用。这里用名词 **translation** 修饰软件。

> **反复出现的语法：叙述过去的经历（回顾交流中的语言笔记）**
>
> 和前面的 **learned to swim**、**thought of my trip** 一样，这段在回忆已经发生的旅行。描述当时的动作时，可用 **take → took、go → went、need → needed、use → used**。
>
> 例如：**“We flew from Shanghai to Osaka and landed at night.”**（我们从上海飞到大阪，夜里抵达。）**Flew** 是 **fly** 的过去式。
>
> **“There are lots of local guys didn’t understand…”** 把两种句型混在了一起。这里直接说 **“Many local people didn’t understand what we said.”** 就可以：**people** 是主语，**didn’t understand** 是谓语，不需要再加 **there are**。
>
> 如果强调现在仍不会日语，**“We can’t speak Japanese”** 也可以成立；如果只描述旅行当时的情况，则用 **“We couldn’t speak Japanese.”** 根据想表达的时间选，不是所有动词都机械地改成过去式。

**Carbon（继续）：**

Second, it's very, it's very interesting to take such, to take such challenge. And second, we tried to take the famous train in Japan from Osaka to Tokyo. I don't know how to speak it in English. It's called Shinkansen in Chinese. The most fantastic part of this was we tried to buy the tickets in single, you know, single tickets. We didn't need the ticket in return because we were going to leave Japan in Tokyo, taking airplane, taking airplane to back to Shanghai. That's it.

> **新干线、单程票、返程（回顾交流中的语言笔记）**
>
> **新干线：the Shinkansen**。英文也沿用这个日语名称；向不熟悉它的人解释时，可以说 **Japan’s bullet train**。你已经说出了英文里可以使用的名称。
>
> **单程票：a one-way ticket**；**往返票：a return ticket**（英式）或 **a round-trip ticket**（美式）。**A single ticket** 在英式英语中也表示单程票，所以你说的 **single tickets** 并没有错；需要调整的是 **“buy the tickets in single”**，可以直接说 **“buy one-way tickets”**。
>
> **“We only needed one-way tickets because we were going to fly back to Shanghai from Tokyo.”**（我们只需要单程票，因为我们打算从东京飞回上海。）这句话解释购票需求，不替你断定购票过程已经完成。
>
> **Take on a challenge** 表示接受／应对挑战；**fly back to Shanghai** 表示飞回上海。原句 **“taking airplane to back to Shanghai”** 中的 **back** 不能在这里当作表示“返回”的动词直接使用。

**Carbon（继续）：**

And the third one, and the third, or maybe thirdly, I'm not sure. Third, and third, I tried to, we took two days in Tokyo and experienced the different things, experienced lots of different things in Osaka, which paid much more money for those, because those things, those stuff are more about shopping and buying something, you know. Oh, finally, all in all, all in all, the trip to Japan is very wonderful, and I think if I have a chance to Japan again, I'm very glad to do so.

> **列点、停留时间和再次旅行（回顾交流中的语言笔记）**
>
> **Third** 和 **thirdly** 都可以用于列出第三点。这里前面用了 **first、second**，接着用 **third** 就很自然，不需要为这个词停下来反复修改。
>
> “在东京待了两天”可以说 **“We spent two days in Tokyo.”**；**“It took us two days to…”** 则表示“我们花了两天完成某件事”，含义不同。
>
> **Stuff** 在这里是不可数名词，可以说 **that stuff**；如果用可数的 **things**，可以说 **those things**。
>
> “如果有机会再去日本，我会很乐意”可以说 **“If I have the chance to visit Japan again, I’ll be happy to go.”** 在表示这种将来可能发生的情况时，**if** 从句用现在时 **have**，主句用 **will** 表示将来的反应。
>
> **后续确认：** 购物主要发生在东京，见下面的问答。原句 **“experienced lots of different things in Osaka”** 以及花费的具体比较关系仍不明确，暂时不改写成确定的城市对比。

**Carbon（继续）：**

Uh, by the way, when I review your question again, I find I might, I am out of the topic, because you were asking what did I do in Osaka, not including the part of Tokyo and the things after that. But I think that's okay.

> **“跑题”和再次出现的从句语序（回顾交流中的语言笔记）**
>
